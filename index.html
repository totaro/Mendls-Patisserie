Our History

Established in 1887 in the heart of Nebelsbad, Mendl's has remained the sole purveyor of exquisite pastries to the Grand Budapest and the wider Republic of Zubrowka for over a century.

Every confection is hand-crafted under the strict supervision of our Master Pâtissier, using only the finest ingredients sourced from the alpine meadows and the royal orchards.

— Herr Mendl
